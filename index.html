Bio

Sidewave is the project of Phil Golyshko, a Colorado-based musician working in shoegaze and alternative rock. Formed in the mid-2010s, Sidewave is built around heavy, fuzz-driven guitars, big choruses, and an immersive field of sound. Over time, the project has evolved with a clear focus on melody, dynamics, and texture. The music is loud and physical, designed to feel expansive without losing impact.
